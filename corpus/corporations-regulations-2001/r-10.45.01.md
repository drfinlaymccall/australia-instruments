## Part 10.45—Application provisions relating to the Corporations Amendment (Statutory Minimum) Regulations 2021

# 10.45.01  Application of amendment of paragraph 5.4.01AAA(1)(b)

The amendment made by item 2 of Schedule 1 to the Corporations Amendment (Statutory Minimum) Regulations 2021 applies in relation to statutory demands served on or after 1 July 2021.
