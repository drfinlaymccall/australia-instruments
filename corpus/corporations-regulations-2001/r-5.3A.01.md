## Part 5.3A—Administration of a company’s affairs with a view to executing a deed of company arrangement

# 5.3A.01  Administrator’s notice of ending of administration

(1) If the administration of a company ends on the happening of an event of a kind mentioned in subsection 435C(2) or (3) of the Act, the administrator of the company or of the deed of company arrangement (as the case may be) must lodge a notice of the happening of the event and the ending of the administration of the company as soon as practicable after the event.
(2) Subregulation (1) does not apply if a notice of the happening of the event is lodged in accordance with the Act or a provision of these regulations other than this regulation.
