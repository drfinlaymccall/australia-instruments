# 10.2.90  ASIC may have regard to prior conduct and events

(1) For a provision of Part 7.6 of the Act mentioned in subregulation (2), ASIC is not precluded from having regard to any of the following matters in exercising its powers and performing its functions under the provision:
(a) a matter that arose at any time before the FSR commencement;
(b) a matter that arose during the transition period;
(c) a matter that arose before a person becomes subject to the amended Corporations Act in relation to:
(i) all of the person’s business; or
(ii) a particular part of the person’s business.

(b) paragraph 913B(1)(c); and
