# 10.27.04  Disclosing information about the AFCA scheme

Regulations 7.9.48, 7.9.48A and 7.9.48B of these Regulations continue to have effect, despite their repeal by item 1 of Schedule 2 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Putting Consumers First—Establishment of the Australian Financial Complaints Authority) Regulations 2018 until the day that standards and requirements are first made or approved by ASIC under subparagraph 912A(2)(a)(i) of the Corporations Act 2001 after those regulations are registered.
