# 7.3.18  Supervisors have qualified privilege in certain circumstances

For section 770C of the Act, a supervisor within the meaning of paragraph 770A (2) (b) of that Act has qualified privilege in respect of:
(a) a statement made by that supervisor, orally or in writing, in the course of the performance by the supervisor of his or her duties under the business rules of the responsible entity; and
(b) notification by the supervisor under paragraph 770A (3) (c) of the Act.
