# 7.13.03  Eligible body: subsection 1097 (1) of the Act

(1) For subparagraph (b) (ii) of the definition of eligible body in subsection 1097 (1) of the Act, Westpac Banking Corporation is prescribed.
(2) For subparagraph (c) (iii) of the definition of eligible body in subsection 1097 (1) of the Act, The Australian Gas Light Company is prescribed.
