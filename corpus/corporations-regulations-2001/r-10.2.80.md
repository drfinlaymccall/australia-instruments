# 10.2.80  Lodgment of Product Disclosure Statement after lodgment of notice

For section 1444 of the Act, if a person lodges with ASIC a notice under paragraph 1438 (3) (b) of the Act:
(a) section 1015B of the Act is taken to authorise the person to lodge a Product Disclosure Statement before the date of effect specified in the notice; and
(b) the period mentioned in section 1016B of the Act is taken to start in relation to the person when the person lodges the Product Disclosure Statement.
