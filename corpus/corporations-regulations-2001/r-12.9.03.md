# 12.9.03  Deregistration

(1) Despite regulation 12.7.02, this regulation applies if the transferring financial institution had not been deregistered before the transfer date as a result of being wound up.
(2) Sections 601AB, 601AC, 601AD, 601AE, 601AF, 601AG and 601AH of the Act apply to the deregistration of the transferring financial institution.
