# 10.52.03  Application—contraventions relating to website disclosure information

The amendments of subregulations 7.6.02A(2) and 9.4AB.02(2) made by Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Delivering Better Financial Outcomes) Regulations 2024 apply in relation to conduct occurring wholly on or after the commencement of Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Delivering Better Financial Outcomes and Other Measures) Act 2024.
