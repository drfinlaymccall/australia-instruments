## Chapter 2A Registration of companies
## Part 2A.1 Size of partnerships or associations (Act s 115 (2))

# 2A.1.01  Size of partnerships or associations

For paragraph 115 (1) (b) of the Act, the number prescribed for a kind of partnership or association is the number specified in the following table for that kind of partnership or association:
Kind of partnership or association | Number
Actuaries, medical practitioners, patent attorneys, sharebrokers, stockbrokers or trade mark attorneys | 50
Architects, pharmaceutical chemists or veterinary surgeons | 100
Legal practitioners | 400
Accountants | 1 000
