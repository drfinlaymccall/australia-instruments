# 10.2.45  Conditions on activity

(1) For section 1444 of the Act, this regulation applies if:
(a) a person is a financial services licensee; and
(b) the person’s financial services licence authorises the person to carry on an activity:
(i) to which Part 7.3 of the old Corporations Act would have applied before that Part was repealed; and
(ii) for which the person would have been required under that Part to have a dealers licence or investment advisers licence that could have been subject to the condition specified in paragraph 786 (2) (d) of the old Corporations Act.
(2) Section 914A of the Act is taken to authorise ASIC to impose the condition specified in paragraph 786 (2) (d) of the old Corporations Act as a condition of the person’s financial services licence.
(3) If ASIC acts under subregulation (2), Part 7.3 of the old Corporations Act, and any associated provisions, continue to apply to the extent necessary to specify the content of the condition specified in paragraph 786 (2) (d) of the old Corporations Act.
(4) Subregulations (2) and (3) cease to apply 2 years after the FSR commencement.
