## Part 10.49—Application provisions relating to Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications and Other Measures) Regulations 2022

# 10.49.01  Application of amendment to Form 509H (creditor’s statutory demand for payment of debt)

The amendment to Form 509H of Schedule 2 to this instrument made by Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications and Other Measures) Regulations 2022 applies in relation to a statutory demand served on or after the commencement of Schedule 1 to that Act.
