# 7.9.11D  Form and content of Product Disclosure Statement for standard margin lending facility

(1) For modified paragraph 1013C(1)(a) of the Act, a Product Disclosure Statement for a standard margin lending facility must include the information and statements mentioned in Schedule 10C.
(2) For modified paragraph 1013C(1)(b) of the Act, a Product Disclosure Statement for a standard margin lending facility must be in the form mentioned in Schedule 10C.
