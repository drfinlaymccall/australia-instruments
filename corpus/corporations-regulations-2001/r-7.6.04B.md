# 7.6.04B  Notification of authorised representatives — basic deposit products

For paragraph 926B (1) (c) of the Act, Part 7.6 of the Act applies as if paragraph 916F (1AA) (d) of the Act were modified by:
(a) substituting the words ‘one or more of’ for the words ‘either or both’; and
(b) omitting ‘paragraph.’ in subparagraph (ii) and inserting ‘paragraph;’ and
(c) inserting after subparagraph 916F (1AA) (d) (ii):
‘(iii) personal advice about a basic deposit product or a facility for making non-cash payments that relates to a basic deposit product.’.
