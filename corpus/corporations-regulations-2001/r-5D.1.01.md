## Chapter 5D—Licensed trustee companies
## Part 5D.1—Preliminary

# 5D.1.01  Prescribed requirements for publication

For the definition of publish in section 601RAA of the Act:
(a) a notice mentioned in paragraph 601WBH(b) of the Act is to be published in the Gazette and on the ASIC website; and
(b) a notice mentioned in paragraph 601WDA(1)(b) or subsection 601WDA(3) of the Act is to be published in a national newspaper and on the transferring company’s website.
