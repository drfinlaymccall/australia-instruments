## Division 3—Certain shares in transferring building societies and credit unions

# 12.8.10  Definitions for Division 3

In this Division:
transferring building society means a transferring financial institution of a State or Territory mentioned in paragraph (a) of the definition of transferring financial institution of a State or Territory in clause 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.
transferring credit union means a transferring financial institution of a State or Territory mentioned in paragraph (b) of the definition of transferring financial institution of a State or Territory in clause 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.
