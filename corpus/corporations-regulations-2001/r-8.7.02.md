# 8.7.02  Futures brokers that are banks — credit to employees to deal in futures contracts

Subsection 1267 (4) of the Act does not have effect in relation to a bank that is a futures broker or a futures adviser only because the bank gives credit to an employee of the bank if:
(a) the credit is given for the purpose of enabling or assisting the employee to deal in futures contracts; or
(b) the bank knows or has reason to believe that the credit will be used by the employee for that purpose.
