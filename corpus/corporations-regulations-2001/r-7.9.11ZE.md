# 7.9.11ZE  Form and content of Product Disclosure Statement for simple sub-fund products

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 1013C(1)(a) of the Act, as modified by Part 5D of Schedule 10A, a Product Disclosure Statement for a simple sub-fund product to which this Subdivision applies must include the statements and information mentioned in Schedule 10F.
(2) For the purposes of paragraph 1013C(1)(b) of the Act, as modified by Part 5D of Schedule 10A, a Product Disclosure Statement for a simple sub-fund product to which this Subdivision applies must be in the form mentioned in Schedule 10F.
