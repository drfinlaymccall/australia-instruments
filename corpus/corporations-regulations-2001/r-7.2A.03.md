# 7.2A.03  Definitions for Division 7.2A.2

In this Division:
compliance period has the meaning given by subregulation 7.2A.08(2).
infringement notice means an infringement notice given under regulation 7.2A.04.
recipient, in relation to an infringement notice, means the person to whom ASIC gives the infringement notice or intends to give the infringement notice under regulation 7.2A.04.
