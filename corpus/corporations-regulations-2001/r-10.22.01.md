## Part 10.22—Application provisions related to the Corporations Amendment (Financial Services Information Lodgement Periods) Regulation 2015

# 10.22.01  Application of amendment of paragraph 7.6.04(1)(c)

Paragraph 7.6.04(1)(c), as amended by the Corporations Amendment (Financial Services Information Lodgement Periods) Regulation 2015, applies in relation to:
(a) an Australian financial services licence granted before, on or after the commencement of that regulation; and
(b) a change mentioned in paragraph 7.6.04(1)(c) that occurs on or after 19 August 2015.
