# 10.2.36  Streamlined licensing procedure for certain regulated principals

(1) For subsection 1433 (3) of the Act, a person mentioned in column 2 of an item in Schedule 10D is not covered by section 1433 of the Act.
(2) If a period is mentioned in column 3 of the item, the person is not covered by section 1433 of the Act for that period.
(3) If a person is described in more than 1 item of Schedule 10D, the person is not covered by section 1433 of the Act in accordance with each of the items.
