# 10.2.130  Continuous disclosure: other disclosing   entities

For section 1444 of the Act, a reference in subparagraph 675 (2) (c) (ii) of the Act to a Product Disclosure Statement, or a Supplementary Product Disclosure Statement, a copy of which has been lodged with ASIC includes:
(a) a disclosure document lodged with ASIC under Chapter 6D of the old Corporations Act; and
(b) a supplementary disclosure document, or a replacement disclosure document, lodged with ASIC under that Chapter.
