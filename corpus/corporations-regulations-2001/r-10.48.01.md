## Part 10.48—Application provisions relating to the Corporations Amendment (Litigation Funding) Regulations 2022

# 10.48.01  Application of amendments relating to litigation funding

The amendments made by the Corporations Amendment (Litigation Funding) Regulations 2022 apply in relation to:
(a) a litigation funding scheme mentioned in regulation 5C.11.01(2A) entered on or after the commencement of those regulations; and
(b) a litigation funding scheme mentioned in regulation 5C.11.01(2A) entered before that commencement, but only in relation to so much of the duration of the scheme that occurs on or after that commencement.

## Part 10.48—Application provisions relating to Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications and Other Measures) Regulations 2022

# 10.48.01  Application of amendment to Form 509H (creditor’s statutory demand for payment of debt)

The amendment to Form 509H of Schedule 2 to this instrument made by Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications and Other Measures) Regulations 2022 applies in relation to a statutory demand served on or after the commencement of Schedule 1 to that Act.
