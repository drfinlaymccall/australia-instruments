# 5.6.74  External administration matters—prescribed countries

For the purposes of subparagraph 581(2)(a)(iii) of the Act, the following countries are prescribed:
(a) the Bailiwick of Jersey;
(b) Canada;
(c) the Independent State of Papua New Guinea;
(d) Malaysia;
(e) New Zealand;
(f) the Republic of Singapore;
(g) Switzerland;
(h) the United Kingdom;
(i) the United States of America.
