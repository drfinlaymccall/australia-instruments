# 7.9.16N  Presentation, structure and format of fees and costs in Product Disclosure Statements

(1) For paragraph 1015C(5)(b) of the Act, the information required by paragraphs 1013D(1)(d) and (e) of the Act must be set out in a single section of the Product Disclosure Statement (fees section) with the heading ‘Fees and other costs’.
(2) The fees section of a Product Disclosure Statement must include:
(a) the Fees and Costs Template, comprising the template and the additional explanation of fees and costs set out in Part 2 of Schedule 10; and
(b) an example of annual fees and costs and associated notes as set out in Part 2 of Schedule 10; and
(c) the boxed Consumer Advisory Warning Statement set out in Part 2 of Schedule 10.
