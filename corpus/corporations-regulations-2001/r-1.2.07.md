# 1.2.07  Restrictions on dealers in share ratio contracts

(1) In this regulation and in regulation 1.2.08:
deal, in relation to share ratio contracts, has the meaning that it has under section 9 of the Act as if the contracts were securities.
(2) The holder of a dealers licence may deal in a share ratio contract on behalf of another person only if:
(a) the holder:
(i) is a member of the Exchange; or
(ii) holds a futures brokers licence; or
(b) ASIC approves, in writing, the holder to deal in those contracts; or
(c) subject to regulation 1.2.09, regulation 8.3.02 applies to the dealing in the contract.
Note The terms dealers licence and futures brokers licence are defined in section 9 of the Act.
