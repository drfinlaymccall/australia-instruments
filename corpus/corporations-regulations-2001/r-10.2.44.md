# 10.2.44  Compensation arrangements if financial services provided to persons as retail clients: section 912B of the Act

(1) For section 1444 of the Act, section 912B of the Act does not apply in relation to a financial services licensee for the period of 2 years commencing on the FSR commencement.
(2) For section 1444 of the Act, if a person:
(a) is a financial services licensee; and
(b) the person’s financial services licence authorises the person to carry on an activity:
(i) to which paragraph 19 (1) (b) or subparagraph 31B (1) (a) (ii) or (b) (ii) of the Insurance (Agents and Brokers) Act 1984 would have applied before that Act was repealed; and
(ii) for which the person would have been required under those provisions to have in force an acceptable contract of professional indemnity insurance;
the Insurance (Agents and Brokers) Act 1984, and any associated provisions, continue to apply in relation to the person to the extent necessary to require the person to have in force an acceptable contract of professional indemnity insurance in relation to the activity.
(3) Subregulation (2) ceases to apply 2 years after the FSR commencement.
