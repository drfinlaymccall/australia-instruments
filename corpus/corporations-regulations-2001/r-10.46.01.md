## Part 10.46—Application provisions for the Treasury Laws Amendment (Greater Transparency of Proxy Advice) Regulations 2021

# 10.46.01  Application of voting advice amendments

(1) The repeal of regulation 7.1.30 by the Treasury Laws Amendment (Greater Transparency of Proxy Advice) Regulations 2021 applies in relation to a service provided on or after 7 February 2022.
(2) Regulation 7.1.28AB, as inserted by the Treasury Laws Amendment (Greater Transparency of Proxy Advice) Regulations 2021, applies in relation to voting advice provided on or after 7 February 2022.
