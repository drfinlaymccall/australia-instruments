# 10.2.79  References to Product Disclosure Statement: managed investment products

For section 1444 of the Act, a reference in the following provisions of the Act to a Product Disclosure Statement includes a disclosure document under Chapter 6D of the old Corporations Act:
(a) paragraph 1012C (6) (b);
(b) paragraph 1012C (8) (c);
(c) subparagraph 1012D (2) (b) (ii).
