# 10.27.03  Written reasons for internal review decisions

Regulations 7.9.48, 7.9.48A, 7.9.48C and 7.9.48D of these Regulations continue to have effect, despite their repeal by item 1 of Schedule 2 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Putting Consumers First—Establishment of the Australian Financial Complaints Authority) Regulations 2018 until the later of:
(a) the commencement of the first legislative instrument made under subsection 47(2A) of the Retirement Savings Accounts Act 1997 as amended by item 7 of Schedule 2 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Putting Consumers First—Establishment of the Australian Financial Complaints Authority) Act 2018; and
(b) the commencement of the first legislative instrument made under subsection 101(1B) of the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993 as amended by item 9 of Schedule 2 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Putting Consumers First—Establishment of the Australian Financial Complaints Authority) Act 2018.
