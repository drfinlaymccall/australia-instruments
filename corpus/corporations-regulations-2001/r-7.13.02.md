# 7.13.02  Marketable securities (Act s 1097 (1))

For paragraph (b) of the definition of marketable security in subsection 1097 (1) of the Act, an interest in a managed investment scheme as defined in section 9 of that Act is a prescribed security if:
(a) it is an interest in a managed investment scheme that is registered under section 601EB of the Act; and
(b) it is quoted on Australian Stock Exchange Limited.
