# 10.2.81  References to responsible person: ongoing disclosure

(1) For section 1444 of the Act, this regulation applies if:
(a) an obligation to provide a Product Disclosure Statement would have existed in relation to a financial product if Division 2 of Part 7.9 of the Act had applied at all times to the product; and
(b) there is no responsible person in relation to the Product Disclosure Statement because Division 2 of Part 7.9 of the Act did not apply at all times to the product.
(2) A reference in subsection 1017B (1) or section 1017C of the Act to a responsible person includes the person who would have been the responsible person in relation to the Product Disclosure Statement if Part 7.9 of the Act had applied.
