# 1.2.27  Modification of Division 1 of Part 8.7 of the Act

In spite of section 1251 of the Act, for sections 1252 to 1257 (inclusive) of the Act, a deliverable share futures contract concerns a body corporate if the contract relates to shares of the body corporate.
Note For the definitions of other terms used in this Division, see section 9 of the Act.
