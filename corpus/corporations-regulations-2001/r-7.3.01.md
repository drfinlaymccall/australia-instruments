## Part 7.3 Participants in the securities industry

# 7.3.01  Licensee to notify events adversely affecting financial position

For subsection 786 (1) of the Act, a licence is subject to a condition that if any event occurs that may adversely affect the financial position of the holder of a licence, the holder must, not later than the day after the day on which he or she becomes aware of it, give notice in writing to ASIC setting out particulars of the event.
