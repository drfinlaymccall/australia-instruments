# 8.4.03  Exemption of certain transactions from subsection 1207 (1) of the Act

Subsection 1207 (1) of the Act does not apply to a transaction that is:
(a) the acquisition of a futures contract before or during a particular month; and
(b) entered into by a futures broker on behalf of a member of a clearing house of a futures exchange;
if:
(c) the futures contract has not been disposed of at the end of that month; and
(d) after the end of that month the broker gives to that member the information that the broker is required, under the business rules of that futures exchange, to give to that member about that transaction.
