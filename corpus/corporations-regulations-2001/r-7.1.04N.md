# 7.1.04N  Specific things that are financial products—litigation funding schemes and arrangements

(1) This regulation is made for the purposes of paragraph 764A(1)(m) of the Act.
(2) The following are declared to be financial products:
(a) an interest in a litigation funding scheme mentioned in regulation 5C.11.01;
(b) an interest in a litigation funding arrangement mentioned in that regulation.
