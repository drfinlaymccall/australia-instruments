# 10.2.97  Registers created after FSR commencement

(1) This regulation applies to a register that:
(a) ASIC is required to create after the FSR commencement under paragraph 922A(2)(b) or (c) of the Act; and
(b) relates to a matter for which:
(i) ASIC had responsibility before the FSR commencement (including a matter that was described in a different way before the FSR commencement); and
(ii) a register (an old register) was in existence under the relevant old law before the FSR commencement.
(2) ASIC must include in the new register as much of the information in the old register as ASIC considers appropriate.
