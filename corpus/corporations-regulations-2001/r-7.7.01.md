## Part 7.7 Registers of interests in securities

# 7.7.01  Register of interests in securities

(1) A register required to be maintained under subsection 881 (1) of the Act may be maintained in accordance with Form 714 or in a manner that enables the particulars required to be disclosed by Form 714 to be readily ascertained.
(2) If the person who maintains the Register is the holder of a licence who carries on business in partnership with another person, the Register must contain particulars of the securities in which the partnership has a relevant interest.
