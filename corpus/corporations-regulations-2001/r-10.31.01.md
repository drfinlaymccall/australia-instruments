## Part 10.31—Application provisions relating to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Corporate and Financial Sector Penalties) Regulations 2019

# 10.31.01  Application—offences

The amendments made by items 1 to 10, items 16 to 18 and items 30, 32, 33 and 37 of Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Corporate and Financial Sector Penalties) Regulations 2019 apply in relation to the commission of an offence if the conduct constituting the commission of the offence occurs wholly on or after the commencement of that Schedule.
