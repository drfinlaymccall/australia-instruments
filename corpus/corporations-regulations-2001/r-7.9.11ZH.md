# 7.9.11ZH  Requirement to provide copy of Product Disclosure Statement for simple sub-fund products free of charge

(1) For the purposes of paragraph 1015C(5)(a) of the Act, this regulation specifies requirements as to the manner in which a Product Disclosure Statement for a simple sub-fund product to which this Subdivision applies must be given to a person if the person requests a copy of the Statement.
(2) The responsible person for the Product Disclosure Statement must give the person, free of charge:
(a) a copy of the Statement within 8 business days; and
(b) a copy of a matter in writing that is applied, adopted or incorporated by the Statement within 8 business days.
