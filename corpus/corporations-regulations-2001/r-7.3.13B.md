# 7.3.13B  Friendly society dealing in interests in friendly society benefit fund

A friendly society in relation to which regulation 7.3.13A applies is taken to be a licensee to which the following provisions of the Act apply:
(a) sections 810, 811, 812 and 815;
(b) Division 4 of Part 7.3;
(c) Division 3 of Part 7.4.
