# 7.7A.12G  General insurance

(1) A benefit is not conflicted remuneration if the benefit is given in relation to a general insurance product.
Note: If a benefit is given in relation to a financial product that consists of both general insurance and life risk insurance, the benefit is to be treated as relating to a general insurance product and a life risk insurance product.
(2) Section 963BB of the Act (about informed consent for insurance commissions) applies in relation to subregulation (1) in the same way it applies in relation to paragraph 963B(1)(a) of the Act.
