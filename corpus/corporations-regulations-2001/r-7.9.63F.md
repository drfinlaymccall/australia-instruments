# 7.9.63F  Confirmation of transaction — acquisition of financial product

(1) For subparagraph 1017F (8) (c) (iii) of the Act, this regulation applies in relation to the confirmation of a transaction that is the acquisition of a financial product.
(2) The details of the transaction relating to the amount paid or payable by the holder in relation to the transaction need include only the amount the holder is required to pay to acquire the product.
