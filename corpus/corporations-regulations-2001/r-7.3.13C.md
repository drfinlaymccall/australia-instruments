# 7.3.13C  Authorisation to deal in interests in certain friendly societies

If a person was, immediately before the transfer date, authorised by a friendly society as described in subregulation 7.3.13A (3), the authorisation is taken to be a proper authority to which the following provisions of the Act apply:
(a) section 814;
(b) subsections 816 (1) and (3).
