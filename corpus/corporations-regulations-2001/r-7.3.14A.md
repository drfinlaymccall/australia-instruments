# 7.3.14A  Exemptions from licensing

Divisions 1 and 3 of Part 7.3, Parts 7.5, 7.6 and 7.7, and section 842, of the Act do not have effect in relation to a friendly society benefit fund that is:
(a) a health benefits fund that is conducted by a registered organisation within the meaning of the National Health Act 1953; or
(b) a superannuation entity within the meaning of the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993.
