## Division 3—Status of directions and notices

# 10.2.08  Direction to securities exchange to comply with ongoing requirements

(1) This regulation applies if the Minister published a notice under subsection 769B(1) of the old Corporations Act before the FSR commencement, directing a securities exchange to do specified things.
(2) On and after the FSR commencement, the notice is taken to be a direction to a market licensee under subsection 794A(1) of the Act.
