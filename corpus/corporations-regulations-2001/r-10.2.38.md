# 10.2.38  Persons taken to be regulated principals: giving of incidental advice and previously exempt persons

(1) For item 9 of the table in section 1430 of the Act, a person is a regulated principal if, immediately before the FSR commencement, the person is a person described in column 2 of one of the items in the following table.
(2) The regulated activities of that person are as specified in column 3 of that item, and the relevant old legislation is as specified in column 4 of that item.
