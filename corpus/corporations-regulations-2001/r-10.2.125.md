# 10.2.125  Responsible entity to be a public company and   hold an Australian financial services licence

(1) For section 1444 of the Act, this regulation applies in relation to a public company that is a regulated principal mentioned in item 1 of the table in section 1430 of the Act.
(2) A reference in section 601FA of the Act to a public company that holds an Australian financial services licence includes the regulated principal.
(3) Subregulation (2) ceases to apply in relation to the regulated principal at the end of the transition period in relation to the regulated principal.
