# 7.5A.112  Withdrawal of infringement notice by ASIC

(1) ASIC may withdraw an infringement notice given by ASIC without an application under regulation 7.5A.111 having been made.
(2) Without limiting subregulation (1), ASIC may withdraw the infringement notice after taking into account a matter mentioned in paragraph 7.5A.111(4)(a), (b), (c) or (d).
