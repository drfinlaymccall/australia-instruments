# 7.3.05  Prescribed form: s 790 of the Act

For section 790 of the Act, particulars of a change in the matters mentioned in paragraph 789 (3) (b) of the Act must be lodged:
(a) by the person who becomes a director or secretary of the body corporate in accordance with Form 704; and
(b) by the holder of the licence in accordance with the approved form.
