## Part 8.2—Foreign offers that are recognised in this jurisdiction
## Division 1—Recognised offers

# 8.2.01  Prescribed offer (Act s 1200C(4)) [see Note 6]

For subsection 1200C(4) of the Act, an offer of a security to which Part 2 of the Securities Act 1978 of New Zealand applies is a prescribed offer in relation to New Zealand.
Note: For the purpose of Chapter 8 of the Act and Chapter 8 of these regulations, a security does not include all of the financial products defined as securities in Part 2 of the Securities Act 1978 of New Zealand—see the definition of securities in section 1200A(1) of the Act. For example, a security does not include an interest in a superannuation scheme or a life insurance policy.
