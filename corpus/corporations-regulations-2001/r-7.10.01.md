## Part 7.10—Market misconduct and other prohibited conduct relating to financial products and financial services

# 7.10.01  Meaning of Division 3 financial products—excluded superannuation products

For the purposes of paragraph (e) of the definition of Division 3 financial products in subsection 1042A(1) of the Act, superannuation products provided by a superannuation entity that is not a public offer entity are prescribed.
Note: This means that these superannuation products are not Division 3 financial products.
