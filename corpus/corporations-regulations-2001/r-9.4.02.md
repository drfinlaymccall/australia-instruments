# 9.4.02  Prescribed penalties (Act s 1313)

For an offence prescribed by regulation 9.4.01 for which a person may be, or has been, given a notice under subsection 1313(1) of the Act, the amount of the penalty is:
(a) if the person is an individual—1.25 penalty units; and
(b) if the person is a body corporate—6.25 penalty units.
