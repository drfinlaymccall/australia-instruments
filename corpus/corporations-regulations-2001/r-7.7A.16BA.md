# 7.7A.16BA  Sale of business

The application of regulation 7.7A.16, 7.7A.16A or 7.7A.16B in relation to a benefit is not affected only because the benefit, or the right to the benefit, is transferred as part of the sale of a business, or a part of a business.
Note: This means that the buyer of a business will have the same protection for benefits that the seller of the business had.
