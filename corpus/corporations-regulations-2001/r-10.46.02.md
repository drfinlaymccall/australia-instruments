# 10.46.02  Application of proxy advice obligations

(1) Subregulations 7.6.03D(1) and (2), as inserted by the Treasury Laws Amendment (Greater Transparency of Proxy Advice) Regulations 2021, apply in relation to proxy advice provided by a financial services licensee:
(a) on or after 7 February 2022; and
(b) under a contract entered into or renewed on or after the commencement of that instrument.
(2) The modifications of Part 7.6 of the Act specified in subregulations 7.6.03D(3) and (4), as inserted by the Treasury Laws Amendment (Greater Transparency of Proxy Advice) Regulations 2021, apply in relation to proxy advice provided by a financial services licensee:
(a) on or after 1 July 2022; and
(b) under a contract entered into or renewed on or after the commencement of that instrument.
