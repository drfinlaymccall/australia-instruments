# 10.2.132  Offers that do not need disclosure: offer to sophisticated investor through licensed dealer

(1) This regulation applies in relation to a regulated principal mentioned in item 1 of the table in section 1430 of the Act.
(2) A reference in subsection 708 (10) of the Act to a financial services licensee includes the regulated principal.
(3) Subregulation (2) ceases to apply in relation to the regulated principal at the end of the transition period in relation to the regulated principal.
