# 10  Compulsory acquistion

Chapter 6A of of the Act applies to all persons as if:
(a) paragraph 661A(4)(c) were modified or varied by, after “if securities”, inserting “or derivatives”; and
(b) paragraph 661B(1)(c)(ii) were modified or varied by omitting the subparagraph and substituting:
“(ii) if the bidder elects under paragraph 661A(4)(c) to acquire securities or derivatives that come to be in the bid class after the notice is given—a holder of the convertible securities or derivatives referred to in that paragraph; and”.
