# 2-2  Circumstance—acquisitions by very large corporate groups

Under subsection 51ABP(1) of the Act, the following circumstances are determined for the purposes of paragraph 51ABO(a) of the Act in relation to an acquisition:
(a) the acquisition is of shares or assets; and
(b) the shares or assets are connected with Australia; and
(c) the acquisition satisfies the very large corporate group revenue test on the contract date; and
(d) the acquisition satisfies the tier-2 acquired shares or assets revenue test on the contract date; and
(e) the acquisition is not covered by Division 2 of this Part.
