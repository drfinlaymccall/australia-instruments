# 1-6  Meaning of connected with Australia

For the purposes of this instrument, a share or asset is connected with Australia if:
(a) in relation to a share—the share is in the capital of a body corporate that carries on business in Australia; or
(b) in relation to an asset that is an interest in an entity (other than a share in the capital of a body corporate)—the interest is in an entity that carries on business in Australia; or
(c) in all other cases—the asset is used in, or forms part of, a business carried on in Australia.
