# 7-22  Application for Tribunal review of acquisition determinations—further information and documents

(1) Under subsection 100D(2) of the Act, this section determines the information and documents that must be given to the Tribunal by a person (the applicant) making an application for the review of an acquisition determination in respect of a notification of an acquisition under subsection 100C(1).
(2) The applicant must give the Tribunal a concise statement that contains:
(a) the principal contentions of fact, economics and law advanced by the applicant in support of the application; and
(b) the material findings of fact, economics and law in the statement of reasons for making the acquisition determination with which the applicant disagrees.
