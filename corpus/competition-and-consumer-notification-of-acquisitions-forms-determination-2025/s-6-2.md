# 6-2  Public benefit application

(1) Under paragraph 51ABZQ(5)(a) of the Act, the form set out in Division 4 of this Part is determined for paragraph 51ABZQ(4)(a) of the Act, in relation to a public benefit application.
(2) Under paragraph 51ABZQ(5)(b) of the Act, the information and documents required to accompany the form, as set out in the form in Division 4 of this Part, are determined for paragraph 51ABZQ(4)(b) of the Act, in relation to a public benefit application.
