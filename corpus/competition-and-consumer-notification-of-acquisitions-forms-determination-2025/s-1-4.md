# 1-4  Interpretation

This instrument incorporates, and is to be read together with, the Competition and Consumer (Notification of Acquisitions) Determination 2025.
Note 1: An effect of this section is that the definitions contained in, and the context and purpose of, the Competition and Consumer (Notification of Acquisitions) Determination 2025 apply to this instrument as if this instrument were part of that instrument.
Note 2: The structure and numbering of the remaining parts of this instrument adopt the structure and numbering from the Competition and Consumer (Notification of Acquisitions) Determination 2025 to assist the reader in reading the 2 instruments together.
