# 51  Taking action in relation to entities and businesses

The following table prescribes values for section 51 of the Act (entities and businesses) to the extent that that section relates to items 2 to 5 of the table in that section.
Threshold value for entities and businesses
Item | For action taken by … | the value (in millions of dollars) is …
1 | an agreement country or region investor where the action relates to: (a) an entity, or a subsidiary of an entity, that is not carrying on a sensitive business or a trustee of a trust that does not hold assets of a sensitive business; or (b) a business that is not a sensitive business | 1 094.
2 | any foreign person in any other circumstances | 252.
Note: A value prescribed by this section is indexed under section 53.
