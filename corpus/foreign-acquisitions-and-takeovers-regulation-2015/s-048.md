# 48  Tracing of substantial interests in corporations, trusts and unincorporated limited partnerships

Subsection 19(3) of the Act does not apply in relation to a significant action or notifiable action taken by a foreign government investor.
Note: The effect of this section is that, for an action taken by a foreign government investor, section 19 of the Act (tracing of substantial interests in corporations, trusts and unincorporated limited partnerships) applies in the circumstances specified in subsection 19(3) of the Act.
