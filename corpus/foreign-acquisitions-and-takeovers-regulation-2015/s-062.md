# 62  Authorised disclosures of periodic aggregate information

(1) For paragraph 124(a) of the Act, the matters that may be disclosed for the purposes of reporting on the administration of this Act are the following:
(a) the number of notices that are given for the purposes of the Act, and the number of those notices that are withdrawn, during a particular period;
(b) the number of no objection notifications imposing conditions that are given during a particular period;
(c) the number of no objection notifications not imposing conditions that are given during a particular period;
(d) the number of applications for variations of no objection notifications that are made, and the number of those applications that are withdrawn, during a particular period;
(e) the number of no objection notifications imposing conditions:
(i) that are varied during a particular period on application; and
(ii) that are varied during a particular period on the Treasurer’s own initiative;
(f) the number of applications for exemption certificates that are made, and the number of those applications that are withdrawn, during a particular period;
(g) the number of exemption certificates:
(i) that are given during a particular period; and
(ii) that are taken to have been given during a particular period;
(h) the number of applications for variations of exemption certificates that are made, and the number of those applications that are withdrawn, during a particular period;
(i) the number of exemption certificates:
(i) that are varied during a particular period on application; and
(ii) that are varied or revoked during a particular period on the Treasurer’s own initiative;
(j) the number of orders that are made under subsection 67(2) (orders prohibiting proposed significant action) or 67(3) (additional orders) of the Act during a particular period;
(k) the number of interim orders that are made during a particular period;
(l) the number of orders that are made under section 69 (disposal orders) of the Act during a particular period;
(m) the number of orders that are varied or revoked under section 71 of the Act during a particular period;
(n) for notices that are given for the purposes of the Act, or no objection notifications that are given, during a particular period—the value of the actions taken or to be taken to which those notices or notifications relate.
(2) A number or value may be specified as a total number or value, or by reference to particular classes, or both.
Note: However, information must not be reasonably capable of being used to identify a person (see paragraph 124(b) of the Act).
